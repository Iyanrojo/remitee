Hi Blue Ink. Your Remitee profile is ready. Things move slowly here, and that's the beauty of it. Here's how to send your first letter:

1
Discover profiles
Each day you can read 5 profiles. Interests and languages first; photos after the match.
2
Make a match
If you both choose each other, the correspondence opens. Whoever completes the match writes first: the app will tell you.
3
Write your letter
On paper, by hand if you like. No rush: you have 7 days for the first letter.
4
Two envelopes, two stamps
Seal the letter in an envelope with only the other person's code. That envelope and a second stamp go inside another one addressed to our office.
5
Mark it as sent
Take a photo of the inner envelope and tap “I've sent the letter”. We'll let you know at every step.
